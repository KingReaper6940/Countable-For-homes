# Real-record demo

1. Open the review queue and note the WPRDC snapshot date and limited coverage.
2. Choose **Trace the overlap** for Bedford. Select **Run live model** when a key is configured (otherwise **View saved analysis**). Read the result's label; a successful API request displays fresh findings, while a failure can display an explicitly labelled saved analysis. Select the sprinkler finding to highlight its exact source quote; use **Back to AI findings** to return. Compare the parent application's 70-unit source claim with separate building permits for A3, A4, A7, and A8. Do not add parent and child counts or call the child subset the full project.
3. In guided step 3, select **Review this link**. Read the sprinkler passage naming `BP-2024-13992`, enter a source-based decision reason, and approve the supporting-trade relationship. The guided card now shows the saved decision, while the housing addition stays unresolved. Refresh the page to show the decision and audit entry persist. If the link was already reviewed, use **See decision** to inspect it.
4. Compare A3 and A4: a shared parcel does not merge distinct buildings. Inspect the temporary-use application, whose `Completed` status coexists with a description saying work under the A3 permit was pending.
5. Open the South 20th Street conversion. The prior City certificate 47881 authorizes an office plus one dwelling; certificate BP-2020-11373, issued February 25, 2024, authorizes two dwellings. Open both redacted City PDFs and compare them with the permit's “ADD A DWELLING UNIT” text. Select the newer certificate, enter `2024-02-25` as the event date, use **Insert cited passage**, complete each evidence check after reading the originals, and record a reason explaining the one-dwelling increase. Approve the event to see +1 in the ledger. This uses genuine City documents; it is a reviewer decision in the local demo.
6. In Bedford, follow the source-investigation links to OneStopPGH and the City's separate occupancy search. Explain that neither a search link nor a permit status establishes occupancy; a reviewer needs a linked original document.
7. Open the ledger and export the CSV and evidence report. The South 20th Street +1 appears only after review, with its City certificate references and recorded reason. Bedford stays unresolved because its selected records lack occupancy proof. Missing evidence there does not mean zero homes were built.

The workflow uses selected real public permit records and two original City occupancy certificates. It does not claim citywide completeness or an occupancy addition from permits alone.
